# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who create, study, or work in digitally noisy environments and want to protect sustained attention.

## Product Purpose

Lumora is a mindfulness and focus app that helps people rise above digital noise, protect their presence, and create with intention.

## Positioning

Lumora presents focus as an immersive, intentional environment rather than another productivity dashboard.

## Operating Context

Prospective users encounter the product through a single-viewport landing experience and can request early access by email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The landing experience is a responsive, single-viewport web surface with no page scroll.
- Visitors can switch among four named ambient scenes.
- The early-access form accepts an email address and provides clear success or error feedback.
- Mobile navigation must be fully usable by touch and keyboard.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Lumora.
- Voice: calm, direct, elevated, and free of productivity hype.
- Binding assets: four supplied ambient videos and one supplied transparent foreground overlay.
- Binding typeface: Instrument Serif for the brand-led display voice; system UI for functional copy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Supplied claim: “Over 10,000 minds already finding their clarity.”
- Supplied statistics: 60+ Deep Sessions, 12,000+ Creators, 4.8 User Satisfaction, Intentional-First Design.
- No customer testimonials, pricing details, or independent validation were supplied; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make calm immediately felt, not merely described.
- Keep the next action obvious and low-friction.
- Let ambience support attention without obstructing content.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and mobile constraints.
